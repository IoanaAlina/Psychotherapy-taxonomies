SUD Treatment Component Dashboard

An evidence-based taxonomy of psychosocial treatment components for substance-use disorders, with the empirical profile structure of 109 randomized trial arms.

Sources & Citations

This taxonomy & profile structure

The empirical results presented here (component frequencies, profile structure, intervention groupings) were developed in:

Preprint: https://doi.org/10.31234/osf.io/bd2yk_v1

Underlying randomized controlled trials

The RCTs analyzed in this work come from two network meta-analyses:

Minozzi S, Saulle R, Amato L, Traccis F, Agabio R. Psychosocial interventions for stimulant use disorder. Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews. Feb 15 2024;2(2):Cd011866. doi:10.1002/14651858.CD011866.pub3
De Crescenzo F, Ciabattini M, D'Alò GL, et al. Comparative efficacy and acceptability of psychosocial interventions for individuals with cocaine and amphetamine addiction: A systematic review and network meta-analysis. PLOS Medicine. 2018;15(12):e1002715. doi:10.1371/journal.pmed.1002715

Definition
Protocol elements

40 unique intervention profiles derived from 109 active arms across substance-use-disorder trials. Each row is a profile; each column is a treatment component (grouped by thematic category, color-coded as in the legend). Nc = number of components present in the profile · Na = number of trial arms represented by the profile. Top: 14 profiles represented by ≥ 2 arms, ordered by Na. Bottom: 26 single-arm profiles, ordered by Nc. Hover any cell, row label, or column label to explore; click a row label to pin the panel and scroll the arms list.
